Transforming the Treatment for an Acute Brain Injury

IV Infusion vs. Oral Doses Every 4 Hours

  • Standard of care is oral nimodipine every 4 hours for up to 21 days. GTx-104 is being developed for IV infusion.
  • Nanoparticle technology enables an aqueous IV formulation of insoluble nimodipine.
    • 100% bioavailable vs. 7.2% oral (Phase 1)
    • FDA Orphan Drug Designation
    • 52 granted & pending patents
